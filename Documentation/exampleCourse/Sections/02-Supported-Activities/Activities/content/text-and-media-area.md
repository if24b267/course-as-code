### 🎨 Text and Media Area
Text and Media Areas can be used to embed formatted content (text, photos, multimedia, code, etc.) into a Moodle course, allowing for the design of nice-looking sections. The content can be formatted as `HTML`, `PlainText`, or `Markdown`.

> 💡 This allows Moodle courses to be made more interesting for students and external resources to be integrated without the need for separate activities.

For more information, see the [Moodle documentation on the Text and Media Area](https://docs.moodle.org/500/en/Text_and_media_area) or this short introductory video:

[Display sound and video in Moodle 4.4/4.5](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=rx5uIHDLM9M)
