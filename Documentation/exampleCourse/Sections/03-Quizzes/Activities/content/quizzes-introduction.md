### 🧠 Quizzes
Quizzes are essentially an activity like any other, but due to their high complexity (compared to other activities), we want to dedicate a separate section to them in this course.

First, read the [documentation on Quizzes](https://davimalu.github.io/course-as-code/Architecture/DataModel/#quiz) and the question formats currently supported by Course as Code carefully, and then answer the questions to test your knowledge!
