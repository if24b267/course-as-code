<h4>input-process-output</h4>
<p>The input-process-output concept is all about taking some input, doing something with it (processing), and then spitting out the result (output). It's important because it's the foundation of how most programs work—whether you're handling user input, crunching numbers, or displaying results, this concept helps you break down and solve problems step by step.</p>
