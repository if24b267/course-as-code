<h3>Selection Structures (if/else)</h3>
<p>Selection structures in Java let you choose different paths in your code based on conditions, like deciding what to do if a number is positive or negative. It's important because it helps your programs make decisions and respond differently depending on the situation, making your code more flexible and smart.</p>

<h4>Relational operators</h4>
<p>Relational operators are used to compare values, like checking if one number is greater than another or if two strings are equal. They're important because they let you make decisions in your code, such as branching into different actions based on whether certain conditions are met.</p>

<h4>Logical operators</h4>
<p>Logical operators help you combine multiple conditions to make more complex decisions in your code, like checking if both conditions are true or if at least one is true. They're important because they let you build more sophisticated decision-making logic, making your programs smarter and more flexible. Think about grading in a course: did you earn enough points AND reached attendance limit?</p>
