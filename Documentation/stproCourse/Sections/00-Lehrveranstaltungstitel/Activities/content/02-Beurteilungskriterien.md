## Assessment Criteria

### Tests and Exams

In addition to possible automatic testing your code is reviewed and graded by humans according to:

1. **compilability (must have)**: if your submission does not compile it is not awarded any points
2. **general case correct**: 50%
3. **edge and corner cases**: 30%
4. **good style**: 20%

### Plagiarism

Plagiarism is academic misconduct and all points of that submission (Exams) are removed.
