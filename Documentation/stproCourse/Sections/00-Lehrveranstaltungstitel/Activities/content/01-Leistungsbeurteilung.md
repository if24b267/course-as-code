<h3>Missing Tests</h3>

<h3>Coaching</h3>

<div class="info-box-blue">
<strong>2 coachings</strong> per student will take place during the course of the semester.
</div>

<p>A coaching is a 1-on-1 meeting with a lecturer. The goal is to assess <strong>learning progress</strong>, identify challenges, and find solutions.</p>

<ul class="spaced-list">
  <li>Contains <strong>practical exercises</strong></li>
  <li>Attend with <strong>working equipment</strong> (PC/Notebook, IDE, ...)</li>
  <li>Duration: approx. <strong>30 minutes</strong></li>
  <li>No points gained or lost - does not influence grade</li>
</ul>

<div class="info-box">
<strong>Important:</strong> Attendance is mandatory. If you do <strong>not attend</strong> at least one coaching, you <strong>lose the first attempt</strong> in the exam.
</div>

<h3>Exams and Retake Exams Overview</h3>

<p>You have <strong>three takes</strong> to pass the course:</p>
<ol class="spaced-list">
  <li>(Tests +) Final Exam</li>
  <li>Retake Exam</li>
  <li>Second Retake Exam (Panel Exam)</li>
</ol>

<p>There are <strong>4 Exam dates</strong>:</p>
<ol class="spaced-list">
  <li>Exam Date</li>
  <li>Second Exam Date</li>
  <li>Third Exam Date</li>
  <li>Fourth Exam Date (Panel Exam)</li>
</ol>

<p>Generally, Final Exam is on Exam Date, Retake Exam on Second Exam Date, etc. If you miss a date due to sickness, you can take that exam on the next available date.</p>

<div class="info-box">
<strong>Note:</strong> Fourth Exam Date is your last chance. It is always a Panel Exam.
</div>

<h3>Retake Exam</h3>
<ul class="spaced-list">
  <li>Available if you don't earn enough points</li>
  <li>Covers all topics</li>
  <li>Grade based on Retake Exam score according to table above</li>
</ul>

<h3>Second Retake Exam (Panel Exam)</h3>
<ul class="spaced-list">
  <li>Available if you don't pass the Retake Exam</li>
  <li>Covers all topics</li>
  <li>Grade based on exam score (bonus points no longer count)</li>
</ul>
